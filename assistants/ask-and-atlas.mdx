---
title: "Use Ask and Atlas"
description: "Explore evidence and prepare drafts while retaining human review."
---

Use **Ask** for a specific question about saved sources. Use the **Atlas** panel to help prepare the next piece of work. Both depend on configured AI access; source inspection and manual review remain available when AI is unavailable.

## Ask about saved evidence

Open **Ask**, enter your question and inspect the answer's numbered citations. Follow them to the original source passages. An answer without citations is unverified. Request details show the saved source/model context where available; older answers may not have that metadata.

Choose **Review as investigation** to bring an answer into a question draft with its supporting context. Review the wording, scope and evidence before creating the question.

## Work with Atlas

Atlas can help you:

- Refine a question.
- Draft a survey or direct request.
- Prepare a video conversation.
- Find useful source passages.
- Suggest the next step.

Its context includes the engagement brief, a selected question, selected source excerpts and recent conversation. Up to five selected sources contribute bounded opening excerpts; this is not a promise that Atlas read every source in full.

Review question or collection suggestions in the actual editor. You can change the draft before saving. Atlas stays alongside the invoking workspace context so you can inspect evidence and return to your work.

## Check the result

Verify citations, scope, attribution and whether the suggestion is still based on current work. Stop or retry a task through the controls shown for it. A stale result needs renewed review. Relevance advice is an aid to judgment, not a truth score.

<Warning>
Answers and drafts do not accept model changes, resolve questions, send requests or establish business approval. Signing out is not a reliable way to cancel already authorized AI work in progress.
</Warning>

Continue through [investigation review](/investigations/review-and-apply) to change the model, or [collection review](/requests/collect-and-import) to gather more evidence.
