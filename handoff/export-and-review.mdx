---
title: "Export and review a handoff"
description: "Download a Markdown account of accepted understanding and remaining work."
---

Open **Engagement** and choose **Export engagement handoff**. The download is a Markdown file that can be read in a text editor or a Markdown viewer.

## What it contains

- Engagement brief and current model revision.
- Accepted model claims, scope and linked evidence identifiers.
- Questions, their status and recorded resolution bases.
- Ask answers and request snapshots where available.
- Reviewed question origins.
- Latest decisions, opportunities and design records, including conditions, ownership and model basis.
- Citation quotes, source versions and available location/provenance details.

The handoff is not a ZIP of original files, a PDF report, a full historical backup or an execution plan. Download originals separately from **Sources** when needed.

## Review before sharing

Check whether the reader can distinguish synthetic material, drafts, operator-recorded accounts and accepted claims. Review stale record warnings, unresolved questions and missing historical metadata. A linked opportunity title is presented as of export; an exact linked opportunity revision may not have been recorded.

Make sure the intended recipient is authorized to receive the information. Exported files contain engagement content and do not retain the workspace's access controls when shared elsewhere.

<Note>
Exporting does not send a message, approve a pilot, execute a query, deploy a system or authorize an operational change.
</Note>

Return to [record review](/records/decisions-opportunities-designs) or [investigations](/investigations/review-and-apply) if the handoff reveals a gap.
