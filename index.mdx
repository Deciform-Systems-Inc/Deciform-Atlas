Deciform helps trained operators investigate how a business works, preserve supporting evidence, and record the decisions and opportunities that follow.

Start with a question that matters to the engagement. Check the original source material, review proposed findings, and explicitly apply the changes that belong in the accepted business model.

## Start here

- [Complete your first investigation](/quickstart) in a synthetic working copy.
- [Understand your workspace and access](/workspace-and-access) before adding company material.
- [Add and review sources](/sources/add-and-review), then [review and apply findings](/investigations/review-and-apply).

## The workflow

| Stage | What you produce |
| --- | --- |
| Sources | Preserved material and exact evidence passages. |
| Questions | Scoped investigations, assessments and proposed changes. |
| Business model | Claims accepted and applied through human review. |
| Records | Decisions, opportunities and solution designs with evidence and conditions. |
| Handoff | A Markdown account of accepted understanding and remaining work. |

[Ask and Atlas](/assistants/ask-and-atlas) help you explore evidence and prepare drafts. Their output remains subject to review. [Requests](/requests/collect-and-import) help collect missing evidence from authorized workspace participants or track collection outside Deciform.

<Note>
The shared reference and examples in this guide are synthetic. Deciform's current workspace supports discovery and delivery planning. It does not execute deployments, certify business approval, or provide a public client portal.
</Note>
