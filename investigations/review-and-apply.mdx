---
title: "Review and apply findings"
description: "Keep evidence assessment, proposal review and question resolution explicit."
---

Use **Questions** for an uncertainty that blocks a decision or meaningful work. Create a question directly, or review a suggestion from [Ask or Atlas](/assistants/ask-and-atlas) in the question editor before saving it.

## Frame the question

Write one focused question and set its branch/time scope. Avoid embedding an assumed answer. A useful synthetic example is “Which role approves an exception at the example branch?” rather than “Why is the approval process broken?”

## Assess evidence

Read relevant sources, save exact citations and write an assessment. With AI enabled, analysis can prepare an assessment and proposed findings. Review the citations and reasoning either way. Preserve contradictions and missing evidence instead of forcing a single conclusion.

Historical assessment links open the requested revision as read-only. Use **Return to current investigation** before reviewing changes or resolving the question. An unavailable revision is not replaced with the latest assessment.

## Propose and review changes

For a manual proposal, choose assessed evidence and describe the business object, field or behavior, proposed claim and optional reason it matters. You can add a claim or supersede an existing claim when its scope matches. Add proposal rows, then save them for review.

Review each change's text, evidence, scope and dependencies. Accept, reject or defer each proposed change. If one change depends on another, inspect that relationship before applying anything.

<Warning>
Accepting a proposal does not update the business model. Use **Apply accepted changes** after completing review. The button includes the number of accepted changes.
</Warning>

A changed model, assessment or engagement brief can make a proposal stale. Follow the review message and recreate or rerun the proposal against the current basis; do not assume it has been automatically updated.

## Resolve separately

After applying changes, inspect them in [Business model](/model/review-versions-and-evidence). Resolve the question only when you can record a supported resolution basis. You can leave it open after applying a partial finding, or [request more evidence](/requests/collect-and-import).

A later assessment can require renewed resolution review. The saved basis records what the resolution relied on; a resolved label is not a guarantee that new evidence has been considered.
