Open **Business model** to inspect understanding that has passed proposal review and been applied. Search for an object or claim and use the scope and version controls to narrow the view.

## Read a claim in context

Check the claim text, business object and field, then inspect its account type, branch and time period. Policy describes a stated rule; practice describes a reported way of working; proposal describes intended or suggested behavior. These accounts may differ without being interchangeable.

Open a linked citation to inspect the exact quote and source version. The evidence map is a way to follow provenance, not a causal graph or proof that the model is complete.

## Inspect an earlier version

Choose the required model version before comparing claims. Exact links retain their record and version context. If a linked claim or version is unavailable, the interface reports that rather than silently showing a different record.

To make a change, return to [Questions](/investigations/review-and-apply) and review a supported proposal. Superseding a claim preserves the earlier model history.

## Check downstream records

[Decisions, opportunities and solution designs](/records/decisions-opportunities-designs) record the model version they used. A newer model can require explicit review of those records before handoff. Do not assume a current model means every downstream narrative is current.
