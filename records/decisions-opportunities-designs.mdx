title: "Record decisions, opportunities and designs"
description: "Keep delivery narratives tied to evidence, conditions and the model they used."
---

Use **Decisions & definitions**, **Opportunities** and **Solution designs** to record the implications of discovery. A solution design must relate to an opportunity.

## Create a useful record

1. Give it a specific title and explain the decision, opportunity or design in its narrative.
2. Link relevant evidence and, where appropriate, an investigation.
3. Record an owner and any conditions or unresolved decisions without inventing agreement.
4. Check the business-model version used as its basis.
5. Save as a draft or recorded account according to what actually happened.

A synthetic opportunity might describe reducing repeated entry in an example dispatch workflow. Its narrative should explain the observed behavior and unknowns; it should not invent quantified savings or an approved implementation.

## Understand status and history

**Draft** means proposed work. **Recorded account** means an operator recorded an account. It is not independent proof of business approval, customer acceptance, implementation or measured results.

Review earlier record versions when following a historical link. Editing creates a new version rather than rewriting the earlier account. If the accepted model changes, review the record's basis before treating it as current. A stale-edit warning requires you to inspect the newer saved version before continuing.

## Prepare for handoff

Check linked opportunities, conditions, ownership and unresolved questions. The [handoff export](/handoff/export-and-review) includes the latest record versions and marks a model basis that needs review. It does not include a full record-history archive or authorize operational work.
