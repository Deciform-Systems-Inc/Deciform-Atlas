| Assessment | A saved interpretation of evidence for a question. |

| Business-model version | A saved version of applied, accepted claims. |
| Resolution basis | The recorded explanation supporting resolution of a question. |
| Recorded account | An operator's record, not independent authentication of approval or implementation. |

## Account scope

**Policy** describes a stated rule or intended requirement. **Practice** describes reported behavior. **Proposal** describes a suggested or intended change. Branch and period identify where and when an account applies. Leave unknown scope explicit rather than filling it with an assumption.

Two accounts can differ because they refer to different branches, periods or account types. Inspect scope before treating a difference as a contradiction or superseding a claim.

## Attribution and uncertainty

A respondent label and the authenticated person who recorded the response are separate. Synthetic scenario history does not represent an actual teammate action. Automated output remains distinguishable from human review. If historical context was not recorded, it stays unknown.

Evidence links show where a claim came from. They do not establish completeness, factual accuracy or agreement on their own. Read the passage and the surrounding source before relying on a claim.
