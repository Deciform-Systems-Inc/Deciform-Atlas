---
title: "Limits and recovery"
description: "Respond to uncertain saves, stale work and unavailable services without losing the review trail."
---

## A save could not be confirmed

A connection failure can occur after the server saved your work. Inspect the current record and use the offered retry or refresh path before creating another copy. For an unchanged draft, retry the existing operation. If you changed the draft, do not treat an older saved result as confirmation of the newer input.

If the result remains uncertain, keep the draft available and ask your Deciform contact to help reconcile it. Avoid repeated fresh submissions. Membership errors similarly require checking the current access list rather than assuming an update succeeded.

## The workspace changed while you were editing

Review the latest saved version and follow the displayed recovery action. Model, assessment and brief changes can invalidate earlier proposal assumptions. Recreate or rerun work against the current basis when required; do not bypass a stale warning simply to preserve the earlier conclusion.

## A draft disappeared

Navigation drafts are held in the current signed-in workspace memory. They are not guaranteed across page reload or sign-out, and some editors discard input when explicitly closed or canceled. Save important work deliberately. A retained draft is not a saved record, and saved AI task history has a separate lifecycle.

## A source cannot be read reliably

Use a supported format, stay within 8 MB and the 500,000-character text limit, and inspect extraction. Scanned PDFs need usable text prepared outside Deciform. Large or complex files may hit additional parser limits. Split material sensibly and preserve enough context for citations. Do not claim an unreadable passage was assessed.

## AI or video is unavailable

AI and video require configured services and appropriate account access. Follow the error shown; manual source review and investigation work remain available when AI is unavailable. Use Direct, Survey or External/manual collection when video cannot be used. Do not infer that a failed generation changed the business model.

Signing out does not reliably cancel already authorized AI work. For video, closing a collection stops new joins and responses but does not immediately terminate an active call; leave the call explicitly.

## A link is unavailable or read-only

Check the selected engagement and your access. Historical assessment links are deliberately read-only; return to the current investigation to edit. A missing exact record is not silently replaced with the latest. Ask the client owner if you need access; do not substitute a different record as evidence.

## What still requires judgment

AI extraction, question wording and relevance advice require practitioner review. Software checks do not guarantee complete evidence coverage, neutrality, customer acceptance or business results. Deciform currently supports discovery and planning; it does not provide a public client portal, public respondent forms, automated meeting transcripts or operational deployment execution.
