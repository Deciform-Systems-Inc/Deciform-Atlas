---
title: "Run a video conversation"
description: "Prepare an agenda and preserve reviewed notes as source material."
---

Video is available when the deployment has its video service configured. Joining requires a signed-in approved account with editor or owner access to the client. Collection links do not provide public guest access. Use another collection method if joining is unavailable.

## Prepare the conversation

Create a Video collection in **Requests**, link the investigation, and write an agenda that leaves room for uncertainty and follow-up questions. Preview and open it when ready. Share its authorized workspace link through your normal communication process.

## Join and capture notes

Join the call from the workspace. Camera and microphone begin off; choose when to enable them. Record notes deliberately and identify the respondent and recorder accurately. Review the notes before saving a response, then [import that response](/requests/collect-and-import) when it is ready to become evidence.

<Note>
Deciform does not provide recording, transcription, a meeting bot, automatic speaker identification or external guest invitations. Saved notes are human-authored records, not an automatically verified transcript.
</Note>

## End collection and leave the call

Closing a collection blocks new responses and new join credentials. It does not immediately end an active call or revoke already issued credentials. Participants should leave the call explicitly.

If a join credential has expired or the call fails, check that the collection is still open and that your account retains access. Reopen the collection page to follow its current join flow. Preserve any unsaved notes before reloading; [navigation drafts are not a durable backup](/reference/limits-and-recovery).
