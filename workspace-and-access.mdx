Sign in at [app.deciform.ai/workspace](https://app.deciform.ai/workspace). Account creation depends on the deployment. When enrollment is available, use your approved team email and complete email verification. Otherwise, ask your client owner or Deciform contact for access. Approval and client membership are separate requirements: creating an account does not by itself grant access to a client's engagements.

## Check context before editing

The workspace identifies your selected client and engagement. **Home** summarizes that engagement and its next actions. Use the engagement selector to switch work, and read the brief in **Engagement** before collecting evidence.

The synthetic reference is read-only. Make or open a working copy to explore editing. Copied example material stays synthetic; subsequent operator work has its own attribution. Access to a working copy follows its client membership.

## Roles

| Role | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Viewer | Inspect the client's engagements and saved evidence. |
| Editor | Save engagement work, including reviewed changes. |
| Owner | Edit work and manage client membership. |

<Warning>
Client access applies to **all engagements for that client**. Check the client name before adding, changing or removing access.
</Warning>

Owners manage access under **Engagement → Client access**. Add an existing verified account by email and choose the role. No invitation email is sent. Tell the person separately how to sign in, using your normal approved communication process. The interface protects against removing your own access through these controls.

## Change the engagement brief

In **Engagement**, choose **Edit engagement**. Saving a new name or brief preserves previous details. Unapplied proposals need to be recreated, and analysis in progress needs to be rerun against the updated brief. If another change arrived while you were editing, review the latest saved details before continuing.

## Drafts and activity

Navigation can retain drafts within the current signed-in workspace. Save important work before reloading or signing out: these drafts are not durable across either action. **Activity** distinguishes actual operator actions, automation and synthetic scenario history.

See [recovery guidance](/reference/limits-and-recovery) if access changes or a save cannot be confirmed.
